# Information Policy

Stable navigation belongs in `START_HERE.md`. Durable product rules belong in `00-Governance`. Adopted reusable assets belong in `10-Resources`. Decisions belong in ADRs. Current status, next work and unpromoted resource candidates belong in `40-State`. Measurements and logs belong in `50-Evidence`.

Nightly answers, notes, desired outcomes and other personal reflections are runtime data. They belong in the browser database or in user-controlled backup files, never in Git, project memory, logs or screenshots committed to this repository. Share packages contain definitions only unless the user makes a separate explicit export choice. Secrets belong outside the repository.
